## 1. Why?

Establish the project's CIFAR-10 DDPM baseline and verify the full training and sampling workflow before adapting it to WBC images.

## 2. Hypothesis

An epsilon-prediction U-Net trained with the reference CIFAR-10 schedule will learn a denoising process that produces recognizable CIFAR-10 samples. The paper's FID 3.17 and Inception Score 9.46 are comparison points, not guaranteed results for this PyTorch/A40 port.

## 3. Setup

- Dataset: CIFAR-10 train split, 50,000 images, 32 x 32 RGB; scaled to `[-1,1]`; random horizontal flip
- Model: base width 128; multipliers `(1,2,2,2)`; 2 residual blocks per level; attention at 16 x 16; dropout 0.1
- Diffusion: 1,000 steps; linear beta `1e-4` to `0.02`; epsilon MSE; fixed-large reverse variance
- Optimizer/training: Adam; learning rate `2e-4`; 5,000-step warmup; gradient norm clip 1.0; batch 128; target 800,000 optimizer steps
- EMA decay: 0.9999; seed: 2026
- GPU: NVIDIA A40; capture actual Slurm job, driver, elapsed time, and peak VRAM
- Evaluation protocol and feature extractor: choose and record before metric computation

FID against train is lower than FID against test, as expected from using the training split as one reference distribution.
